# examples/reels

Sample output of the app's **Make my reel** button (`POST /api/reel`, `app/stitch.py`): four real MiniMax H3 clips from `../real/` (forest-cabin FPV drone, forest-cabin dolly-in, coast-lighthouse orbit-right, alley crane-up) cut together with 0.4 s crossfades and a burned-in "REAL: <model> via Comfy Router" label, once at 16:9 (1280x720) and once at 9:16 (720x1280, blurred-pad fit of the 16:9 footage; no AI outpainting). ffmpeg only: no new generation, no credits spent. Simulated clips are skipped by the reel button. Each `.recipe.json` lists the clips used.
