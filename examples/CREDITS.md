When real outputs exist (LTX-2 on a GPU, or Router), commit them under `examples/outputs/real/` with the recipe the app writes (engine, model, prompt, seed, workflow hash) and list them here with the model that produced them and its licence. Do not relabel simulated files as model output.

Third-party software: FastAPI, Uvicorn, Pydantic, Pillow, NumPy, httpx, jsonschema, `comfy-sdk` (see each project's licence). ffmpeg is invoked as an external program and is not bundled.
